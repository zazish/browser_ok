# browser_ok
browser_ok is a VERY lightweight (169 mb yeah very lightweight) browser that works just like any browser, like it has tabs etc. since its very lightweight (sure yeah) its kind of usable as daily driving it. has capable of playing youtube videos at a high framerate and good quality (tested on the hey its me its verity video). also capable of downloading files and stuff etc. doesnt have that detailed of an ui. it also runs on webview 2. by what i know to run this since its on webview 2 you must have webview 2 installed on your pc (or the setup might just install it idk)
